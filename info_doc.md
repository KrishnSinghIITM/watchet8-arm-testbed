ABOUT THE INITIATIVE
Namma AI Initiative
The Namma AI Initiative aims to train and upskill 2,000+ students and developers in Edge AI and SoC-based computing technologies, contributing to the development of a future-ready talent pool in AI, embedded systems and semiconductor-related technologies.

It is a national-level initiative & jointly launched Hackathon by Arm and IIIT Bangalore in collaboration with NASSCOM. The initiative is supported by the UK Government through the UK-India Technology Security Initiative (TSI).

The initiative seeks to create a scalable AI skilling and innovation platform, bringing together industry experts, academic institutions and emerging developers to strengthen technology talent pipeline. The objective is to build practical competencies in Edge AI, on-device AI deployment, embedded computing and hardware–software optimisation using Arm-based technologies.

Students and developers are invited to register to the program. All registered participants would get invitation to participate in this Industry-academia skilling and innovation programme, offering structured learning opportunities on Edge AI, (both in-person and virtual option). Certificates would be given to everyone successfully completing these trainings.

The shortlisted hackathon finalists would be provided with a focused virtual technical training ahead of the hackathon to strengthen their readiness to develop functional Proofs of Concept (PoCs). All participants would receive program branded mementoes and certificates. Winners of this hackathon would receive cash awards and other exciting awards.

Namma AI Initiative seeks to strengthen Karnataka's position as a hub for AI talent development and contribute not only to state’s but nation’s growing AI and semiconductor technology ecosystem. This will strengthen engagement between students, developers, academic institutions and technology experts, creating pathways for continued learning, mentorship and industry exposure.

Beyond a one-day hackathon, the initiative is envisioned as a three-month, industry-led skilling and innovation programme, offering structured learning opportunities on Edge AI through a combination of scheduled virtual and in-person technical sessions delivered by industry experts.

PROBLEM STATEMENT 1
Domain: Education | Digital Inclusion | Accessible AI
AI for Inclusive Education – Offline Smart Learning Assistant
Societal Challenge
Students in underserved and low-connectivity regions may face barriers to learning, including limited internet access and a lack of timely, understandable study support. Can affordable offline Edge AI help overcome one clearly identified learning barrier?

Hackathon Task
Build an offline learning assistant using ExecuTorch on Raspberry Pi. Identify a specific learning-access barrier (for example, inability to access study support without connectivity) and show how locally generated learning aids help address it. For this one-day challenge, the demonstrable intervention is offline educational content support—not a complete education platform.

Minimum Deliverables (Mandatory)
✓
Identify one learning barrier, the intended learner group and how the PoC addresses that barrier.

✓
Process one organiser-provided educational passage (approximately 150–300 words) with an approved lightweight model running locally through ExecuTorch on Raspberry Pi.

✓
Generate a short, understandable summary and at least three relevant quiz questions as an accessible, offline study aid. Terminal output is sufficient.

✓
Demonstrate operation without cloud AI inference; report approximate inference time, model used and model size or memory usage where measurable.

✓
In a brief impact explanation, show how the generated aids could reduce the chosen learning barrier, with one concrete example from the supplied passage.

Expected Outcome
A working offline PoC that converts a short passage into a summary and three quiz questions, accompanied by a credible demonstration of how this supports learners facing the identified access barrier.

Not required
Full chatbot, multilingual support, document management, custom interface or mobile app.

PROBLEM STATEMENT 2
Domain: Public Safety | Smart Infrastructure | Computer Vision
AI for Safer Communities – Edge-Based Object Detection and Counting
Societal Challenge
Public spaces, roads and community infrastructure need affordable ways to understand vehicle or pedestrian movement without expensive computing or continuous cloud connectivity.

Hackathon Task
Build a lightweight object-detection PoC using ExecuTorch on Raspberry Pi to identify and count objects from a supplied short video or images. Choose an application such as traffic observation, pedestrian monitoring or public-space utilisation.

Minimum Deliverables (Mandatory)
✓
Process one organiser-provided 10–20-second clip or 10–20 sample images using an approved pretrained object-detection model on ExecuTorch and Raspberry Pi.

✓
Detect at least one predefined object category (for example, people or vehicles) and display counts for each selected image or frame. Terminal output or saved annotated images is sufficient.

✓
Submit category and counts plus at least two sample detection outputs.

✓
Report average inference time per frame/image, model used and approximate throughput where feasible.

✓
Explain the societal issue, how detection and counting may help, and one practical application (maximum 2 minutes).

Expected Outcome
A functional local Edge AI object-detection PoC that identifies and counts selected objects in supplied visual data.

Not required
Live camera, real-time surveillance, tracking, complex dashboards, cloud connectivity or mobile applications.

PROBLEM STATEMENT 3
Domain: Sustainability | Waste Management | Computer Vision
AI for Sustainability – Edge-Based Waste Sorting and Food Categorisation
Societal Challenge
Improper segregation of waste and limited visibility into food and other discard categories can hinder responsible disposal and resource recovery. Can affordable, offline Edge AI help identify everyday waste categories to support better sorting decisions?

Hackathon Task
Build a lightweight image-classification or object-detection PoC using ExecuTorch on Raspberry Pi that categorises items in organiser-provided images for a practical waste-management use case. Teams may focus on waste sorting (for example, recyclable versus non-recyclable) or food/waste categorisation, using the organiser-defined labels.

Minimum Deliverables (Mandatory)
✓
Use 10–20 organiser-provided labelled images representing at least two organiser-defined categories, with a verified pretrained model running locally through ExecuTorch on Raspberry Pi.

✓
Produce a predicted category for each image (and, if using object detection, indicate detected items). Terminal output or saved annotated images is sufficient.

✓
Provide a simple results table with predictions against supplied labels, total correct classifications and accuracy; show at least two sample outputs.

✓
Report model used, approximate inference time per image and model size or memory usage where measurable.

✓
Explain one realistic sorting or categorisation scenario, who benefits and how offline categorisation could improve waste-handling decisions (maximum 2 minutes).

Expected Outcome
A functional offline Edge AI PoC that categorises supplied waste or food-related images, reports basic classification results and demonstrates a plausible sustainability application.

Not required
Physical sorting mechanism, robotics, live camera integration, training a new model complex dashboards or a production-grade waste-management system.
